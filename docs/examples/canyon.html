Canyon relief

Difficulty: easy

Steps

  1. Draw a rectangle over the basemap, project it (-proj merc) and click + New frame.

  1. Load a DEM: -fetch 3dep-1m

  1. Make a hillshade with the hillshade panel.

  1. Load imagery: -fetch naip

  1. Remove shadows from the imagery: -deshadow dem=3dep-1m

  1. Adjust the colors of the imagery with the adjust colors panel.

  1. Blend the hillshade with the imagery using the blend hillshade panel.

For details on each step, see the Shaded relief guide.
